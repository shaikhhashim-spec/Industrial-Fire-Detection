Not available for all of India

Investigations needs a full OpenStreetMap join and rule classification. In the dashboard itself these only run for the detailed Jharkhand and Odisha belt — running them for the whole country on every load would be far too slow, so the India-wide view you see here does not have them either, on the public site or in the local app.

To open Investigations, run the app locally (python start_all.py, then http://localhost:8085) and switch Region in the top bar to the Jharkhand and Odisha belt.

What it shows, once open
  • Persistent clusters ranked by days active, with detection counts, mean and peak FRP
  • Why the classification rule fired, and whether the ML model's second opinion agrees
  • Distance to the nearest mapped industrial site, mine, power plant, forest and water body
  • Whether this run's FRP is anomalous against the cell's own historical baseline, and its FRP history charted over time
  • Analyst Mode: the raw feature values behind the classification, for a second check
